# TPP Group

> TPP Group is a media intelligence company that creates and scales Arabic content, helping brands make data-driven marketing investments. It operates four integrated divisions - Network (Arabic IP development, 40+ owned and operated properties), Studio (production, 1,400+ episodes), Agency (performance-driven distribution), and Intelligence (psychographic audience-behavior analytics and attention measurement). A 500 Global portfolio company. No public developer or API surface is published (checked 2026-07-21).
